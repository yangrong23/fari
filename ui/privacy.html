Privacy

Your photos stay yours.

fari turns a single photo into a short AI video — and nothing more. This page explains, in plain language, how we keep your photos anonymous, used only for your video, and fully under your control.

🔒 Your protections at a glance

  • 🔒No account and no name needed — every upload is anonymous.
  • ✓Your photo is used only to generate your own video.
  • ✓Original uploads are automatically deleted within 24 hours.
  • ✓We never sell your photos and never share them with advertisers.
  • ✓You can request full deletion of your data at any time.

Information we collect

We collect the minimum needed to make your video — nothing extra.

  • The photo you choose to upload, so we can generate your preview and final video.
  • The template and the settings you select.
  • Whether a payment was completed, as confirmed by the payment provider — we never see your card number.
  • Basic device and browser information, used only to keep the site secure, stable, and working.

How we use information

We use information only to generate your video, process your payment, keep the site running, prevent abuse, and improve quality. Each purpose uses the least data necessary, and nothing is used to build a profile of you.

How your photos are handled

This is the part that matters most, so we keep it simple:

  • Your uploaded photo is used only to create the video you requested.
  • It is never posted publicly, added to a gallery, shown to other users, or used to train AI models.
  • Original uploads and the files created to deliver your video are automatically deleted within 24 hours.
  • We do not sell and do not share your personal media with anyone.

Payments

Payments are handled by the payment provider. fari never receives or stores your full card number or wallet credentials — only a confirmation that the payment succeeded.

Cookies and tracking

We use a minimum of cookies for the site to function and to understand basic usage so we can improve it. When measurement tools (such as analytics or ad conversion tags) are enabled, they receive only aggregated, non-identifying data. You can switch them off any time through your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you. To protect you, we may verify the request before acting on it — then we act on it promptly.

Contact

Privacy questions are welcome through the Contact page. We aim to reply to every privacy request within a few days.
